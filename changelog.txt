New:

Your cat can keep it to themselves, confront the cat who hid it, or look for their birth family at the next Gathering. A new setting in the Warrior code tab, "Birth secrets come out rarely", makes it 3 times rarer. Kits taken at 3 moons or older remember
- Relationships with more going on:
  - Wants: every cat from 6 moons up wants something (an apprentice, a mate, kits, a friend, someone's respect, to see the world, to get even...). It shows on the profile of your cat and of cats your cat knows well, and your cat can change theirs every 6 moons from the "your tab" buttons. A want that comes true makes a cat happy, one that waits 12 moons makes them restless
  - Ambition: ambitious cats resent the deputy, and the most ambitious ask the leader for the deputy's place (your choice if your cat is the leader)
  - Jealousy: of a mate's crush, a mentor's new apprentice, a littermate made warrior first, or a cat who got the role they wanted. A jealous mate may even end things
  - Moods: grieving, bitter, restless, lonely, happy or content, worked out every skip (every week with weeks on). Moods nudge temptations and how cats get along, and your cat has short mood events now and then
  - Two new settings: Ambition and Jealousy (both on)
- Friendships: cats who like and feel at ease with each other become friends, and the closest become best friends (one at a time). Friends spend time together, are picked together for patrols when the game picks and do a little better on them, keep each other's misdeeds quiet more often, and speak up for mercy at judgments if they're the deputy or a senior warrior. A cat grieves a best friend longer and remembers them on the profile. Friends can drift apart or fall out (and want to make up), and a best friend can get jealous of a new best friend or mate. Your cat's profile shows their friends and best friend
- Families: parents, kits and littermates can be close, ordinary or distant. Close parents are proud at their kit's ceremonies, sit with a hurt or sick kit in the healer's den, and like a cat less for picking on their kit. Littermate apprentices grow closer, unless one is ambitious or competitive, then they may become rivals. A distant parent and grown kit drift apart, and ones who dislike each other become estranged (no patrols together when the game picks), until one of them wants to make up. In multi clan mode, cats are glad to see close family at Gatherings, may want to see them again, and hear there when one dies. Elders tell stories to their grandkits. Your cat's profile shows who in their family they're close to, distant from or estranged from
- Breed presets in the cat maker (a player suggestion): a new Breed button next to Random cat lists Genemod's 72 breeds (common, uncommon and rare). Picking one makes a random purebred cat of that breed, and picking it again rolls another. Only the looks change, and the cat's kits inherit the breed
- New talk lines with other Clans' cats (a player suggestion): a beach Clan's cats get teased about their fishy smell by cats from Clans that aren't on the beach, a shell left at the border for a cat your cat is starting to like, and a mate from another Clan torn up when your two Clans are at war
- Talk with other Clans' cats has more going on: many lines are little scenes now, some with a choice that changes how the other cat feels about your cat, and a cat with a crush may leave your cat a feather
- A new Buttergen tab in the Clan Settings holds Buttergen's own settings: weeks, warrior code events, ambition and jealousy

Changes:
- Brought up to date with Genemod from November 24, 2026: the leader's den can send aid (some of the Clan's prey and herbs) to another Clan, and works in multi clan mode too. A Clan's temperament now follows the cats it has, and the mediation screen is fixed

<strong>Buttergen v0.4-dev.1: the honor system</strong>

A development build, so keep a copy of your saves folder before playing.

The honor system:
- Every cat has honor (what they really did), reputation (what the Clan knows) and, for leaders, deputies and healers, Clan trust. Profiles show them as labels with the number, like unremarkable (+6)
- Cats are tempted to break the warrior code by what's going on around them: hunger, a crush in another Clan, a grudge, their personality and Dark Forest ties. Every role has its own ways to break and follow the code
- Someone may see: witnesses report it, keep the secret or hold it over the cat, and secrets can come out later through gossip, a falling-out or a deathbed confession
- The leader judges at the full moon: a warning, extra duties, lost trust, flavor demotion, shunning or exile, depending on the leader, the deputy and how the Clan feels about the cat
- Your cat's choices: for big temptations and for misdeeds your cat sees, a "Your cat has a choice" button shows on the events screen. The skip never stops, and if you skip on, your cat decides by personality
- Your cat can be exiled, ask to come home later or, in multi clan mode, ask another Clan to take them in
- An optional Clan setting makes healer mates break the code, with secret kits that can come out later
- Leaders break and follow the code too. Their misdeeds cost Clan trust, StarClan can send omens, and a Clan that has lost all trust can challenge its leader
- Multi clan mode: cats can switch Clans the warrior code's way, by asking a leader at a Gathering and doing a task for the new Clan
- Cruel season cards: Heartless, Weakened Stars, Forsaken and Anything For The Throne touch honor, and five new cards: Iron Code, Fading Code, Whispers, Watchful Stars and No Way Back
- Clan settings for rare or frequent warrior code events, and honor, reputation and Clan trust in the cat editor

New:
- Brought up to date with Genemod's modded branch and Clangen's development branch from November 8, 2026: Clangen's new healer den screen, the On Their Own, Hostile Environs and Healer's Tribute cruel season cards, and more
- Your cat's family fits your cat: Clan cats are only picked as your cat's birth parents when they could have had your cat, and siblings look more like your cat
- Cat maker: accessories can be picked again, the scar checkmarks follow every change, and the accessory list opens quickly

Fixes:
- Fixed a crash when kits are born (new with the November 5 update)
- A cat that comes back to the Clan partway through a moon starts out fed (it was skipped with an error in the log)
- The cat maker's little white patches, old style tortie patches and Mink's Torties show up right with Clangen's new split patch sheets
- The README explains what to do if setup stops with "failed to hardlink file" (the game folder is or was inside OneDrive)
